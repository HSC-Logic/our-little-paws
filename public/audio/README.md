# Our little soundtrack

`for-you-october-25.opus` and `for-you-october-11.opus` are personal vocal recordings supplied by the project owner, preserved unchanged from the WhatsApp audio files dated October 25 and October 11, 2026. They are not generated instrumentals. These files ship with the app and are accessible to anyone who can access its static assets; keep deployment access appropriate for personal recordings.

These three instrumental WAV loops are original procedural compositions created for this application by `scripts/generate-audio.mjs`. They use synthesized sine harmonics, with no external samples or recordings. The project author may use, modify, and distribute these generated audio assets with the application.

Additional unchanged personal recordings: `for-you-july-30.opus`, `for-you-june-30.opus`, `for-you-june-3.opus`, `for-you-may-31.opus`, and `for-you-may-30.opus`, from the corresponding WhatsApp audio files dated in 2026. The same personal-recording access considerations apply.

Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-audio.mjs`. Playback begins after a Play gesture and remains local to each device. Shared track selection does not synchronize playback position.
